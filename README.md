## About Me

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate with a strong academic record and a genuine interest in building software that works well and is written with care.

My focus areas are **data structures & algorithms** (daily practice in Java), **software development** (desktop, web, and systems), and an emerging foundation in **data science and machine learning**. I document everything I learn — on GitHub and on my technical blog — because writing forces understanding.

I work with discipline. I track what I build, revisit what I get wrong, and ship things regularly.

> *"I'm not competing with others — I'm competing with who I was yesterday."*
